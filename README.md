# Your Music

We all love Spotify Wrapped, but it only shows you what Spotify wants you to see, once a year. **Your Music** gives you full access to your complete listening history on your own terms.

Search any song and see exactly when you first heard it, how many times you've played it, and how your listening has changed over time. Browse your all-time or year-by-year top tracks ranked by play count or total minutes.

**To use it:** download your Extended Streaming History from Spotify's privacy settings, zip the folder, and upload it. Your data never leaves your browser.

---

**Built with** Python, Streamlit, Pandas, and Plotly.
